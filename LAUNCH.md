## LinkedIn

> I just shipped PantryPin v0.1.0 🍽️
>
> It started with a practical question: after seeing a meal, could one photo help you understand what may be in it, roughly how nutritious it is, what the ingredients might cost, and how to recreate it?
>
> PantryPin connects those questions in one workflow:
>
> • likely ingredient breakdowns
> • calorie ranges and macro estimates
> • confidence labels for uncertain inferences
> • ZIP-sensitive simulated basket comparisons
> • cooking-resource searches
>
> The public GitHub Pages build is a labeled scenario demo. Developers can self-host the included OpenAI vision route for real image analysis. Retailer figures remain simulated—not live price or inventory data.
>
> Live demo: https://nahin333.github.io/where-and-how-much/
> GitHub: https://github.com/nahin333/where-and-how-much
>
> I would especially value feedback on two questions: Is the distinction between inferred and verified information clear? Which part would make this genuinely useful?
>
